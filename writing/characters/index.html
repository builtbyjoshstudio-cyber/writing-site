The Five Friends of Overlayed Echoes

Five friends spent fifteen years rolling dice around the same tables. Then they built a game that made the table real — and stepped inside. Meet them as they were in the hour before the first session: the game master, the guardian, the healer, the trickster, and the mage.

The friends

  • Kael The Game Master The quiet one who built the game — and holds the story together.
  • Theo The Guardian The protector who counts the exits before anyone steps inside.
  • Angela The Healer The nurse who settles a room — kind but sharp; Theo's sister.
  • Marcus The Trickster Chaos personified; the laugh that keeps everyone from getting scared.
  • Lena The Mage Quiet and watchful, all explosive magic — Kael's sister.
